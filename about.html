A little about me

Meet Dee.

Hey, I’m Deniqua, but you can call me Dee!

I’m a mom of twins, a cybersecurity professional, and someone who genuinely enjoys learning new things. My journey into cybersecurity wasn’t exactly a straight line, but looking back, I wouldn’t change much about it.

My background includes operations, networking, and IT, and somewhere along the way, I decided cybersecurity was where I wanted to be. I went back to school, earned my bachelor’s degree in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance, picked up quite a few certifications along the way, and I’m currently working on my master’s.

I created Dee’s Cyber Corner because I wanted a space to share my journey beyond a résumé or a LinkedIn post. Somewhere I could document what I’m working on, talk about things I’ve learned, share resources that helped me, and maybe help someone who’s trying to figure out their own path.

You’ll find a little bit of everything here: cybersecurity projects, hands-on labs, resources, blog posts, and lessons I’ve picked up along the way. I’m still learning myself, and that’s part of the fun.

And yes, I do have a life outside of tech! I enjoy reading, building LEGO sets, and making time for self-care. Sometimes I just want to relax, clear my mind, and enjoy the moment without thinking about work, school, or what I need to do next. I’m big on faith, personal growth, and finding balance along the way.

I don’t have everything figured out, and I don’t pretend to. I’ve had my share of setbacks, changed directions a few times, and learned a lot about myself in the process.

If there’s one thing I hope you take away from being here, it’s that your journey doesn’t have to look like anyone else’s.

Welcome to my little corner of the internet.
