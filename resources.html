01As someone with ADHD, this is what helped me The study methods that worked for me

I have ADHD, and throughout college I had to discover study techniques that helped me stay focused, understand concepts, and retain information. These are the methods that personally helped me.

  • Pomodoro Method

    I used focused study sessions with breaks in between instead of trying to study continuously for hours.

  • Active Recall

    I would study a topic, put my notes away, and try to remember what I had learned without looking at the answers. This helped me identify which concepts I understood and which ones needed more attention.

  • Teaching Myself the Lesson Back

    After learning something, I would explain it back to myself in my own words, almost as though I were teaching someone else. This helped me understand concepts rather than simply memorize them.

  • Creating My Own Quizzes

    I would create practice quizzes based on what I was studying. Quizzing myself helped me test my understanding, remember information, and identify weak areas.

  • Short Study Sessions

    I found that studying in shorter sessions worked better for me than forcing myself to sit for long periods. I focused on manageable amounts of information at a time.

02How I prepared for certifications My step-by-step process

I hold over 12 industry certifications, so I feel like I’ve got cert prep down (until they change the exams, lol). When it came to certification, this is what I did.

  1. Search Reddit and DiscordI search Reddit and Discord for tips and to gather the study notes and resources people used when they passed. I’m currently in college, so these communities have been a big help.
  2. Download the official exam objectivesThe objectives tell you exactly what can be on the exam. Everything on them is up for grabs.
  3. Turn the objectives into a study planI put the objectives into an AI tool like ChatGPT or Claude to create a study plan and clear up any gray areas. While I study, I have it break information down so simply that a toddler could understand it. You’re taking in a lot, so make it digestible for you.
  4. Pair my notes with videosOnce I have my study plan and notes, I pair them with YouTube videos. For Security+, I used Professor Messer’s videos, and he has study materials too.
  5. Prefer not to use AI? Use reputable study materialIf you’d rather not use ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI tools, look at who offers trusted study material. I’ve used Jason Dion and Professor Messer, and there are plenty of others. Do your research.
  6. Don’t overcomplicate itSee what’s being covered, study, take practice tests, and give it a shot.

If you don’t pass

It isn’t the end-all be-all. I’ll let you know now: I have failed certifications multiple times, and guess what? I’m still in the industry. Go back to the drawing board, make some adjustments, and execute.

You got this. If it was easy, everyone would be doing it.

03College survival tips Lessons from finishing college with ADHD

Lessons from completing college while managing ADHD and other responsibilities.

  • Finding study methods that fit how you learn.
  • Breaking assignments and difficult topics into smaller pieces.
  • Using short study sessions to avoid feeling overwhelmed.
  • Taking breaks when needed.
  • Testing your knowledge instead of only rereading material.
  • Explaining concepts out loud to improve understanding.
  • Finding supportive learning communities.
  • Staying consistent without expecting perfection.
  • Remembering that everyone’s learning journey is different.
