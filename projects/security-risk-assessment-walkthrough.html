Security Risk Assessment & Remediation

I assessed security gaps against NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5, considered their impact on confidentiality, integrity, and availability, and developed remediation recommendations and a POA&M process.

1. Identify gaps and impact

Assess control deficiencies and consider confidentiality, integrity, and availability using the project’s NIST and FIPS context.

2. Analyze the selected controls

Review AC-6, CA-5, CA-7, RA-3, and RA-7 and connect each control to the gaps being assessed.

3. Develop access recommendations

Recommend role based access, least privilege, and periodic reviews to reduce excessive access.

4. Track remediation through POA&M

Define severity, ownership, deadlines, status, and evidence of completion for remediation work.

5. Document the policy context

Connect the point of sale security policy to PCI DSS requirements and assign technical ownership.
