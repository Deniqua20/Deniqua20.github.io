What I assessed

Control deficiencies and their potential impact on confidentiality, integrity, and availability. My write up focuses on AC-6, CA-5, CA-7, RA-3, and RA-7.

What I recommended

Access governance, periodic access reviews, risk response, and a remediation process with severity, ownership, deadlines, status, and completion evidence.

What I documented

A PCI DSS aligned policy for a point of sale environment, covering network security, secure configurations, default credentials, malware protection, and technical ownership. The public repository contains the project summary.
