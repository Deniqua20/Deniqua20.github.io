How your matches work

Each area has three tasks with equal importance. Interest levels run from 0 for “Not for me” to 4 for “Very interested.” “Not sure yet” is excluded. An area needs at least two rated tasks and an average of “Somewhat interested” or higher to appear in your results.

We compare the average interest within each area. The three strongest positions are shown, including any areas tied at the cutoff. Equal scores are treated as equally interesting. These are comparisons of your answers, not probabilities, ability scores, or employment forecasts.

The questions and scoring are original to Dee’s Cyber Corner. For a broader established career interest tool, try the O*NET Interest Profiler. Explore cybersecurity roles with NIST NICE, CISA’s career pathways, and CyberSeek. These organizations do not endorse this questionnaire.
