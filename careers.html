The career observatory

Paths in Tech.
See what’s possible.

There’s more than one way into tech, and no single right path. Explore what’s out there: the teams, the work they do, and the roles you’ll see.

Blue team

Defend and investigate

The defenders. They watch for threats, respond to incidents, and protect accounts and systems.

Red & purple team

Test and strengthen

Red teams test defenses the way an attacker would, with permission. Purple teams connect what attackers find to how defenders and builders fix it.

White team

Rules, risk, and design

The planners. They set the rules, measure risk, and design how security fits together.

Yellow team

Build and create

The builders. They create the software, data systems, networks, and experiences everyone else protects.

The team behind the teams

Help and connect

Every team needs people who support users, explain technology, and help it get adopted.
