Teams 101

Meet the teams

In cybersecurity you’ll hear about the red, blue, and purple teams. Here’s who does what, plus other cyber paths and tech paths that aren’t cyber. You don’t need to pick one forever. Lots of people move between teams over their career.

Blue team

Cyber Defense

Think of them as the security guards and detectives of a company.

  • Watch alerts for anything suspicious
  • Investigate odd logins and strange activity
  • Stop an attack and clean up after it
See their paths ↓

Red team

Offensive Security

Think of them as burglars you hire, with permission, to test your locks before a real one shows up.

  • Try to break in the way an attacker would
  • Find weak spots before the bad guys do
  • Write up what they found and how to fix it
See their paths ↓

Purple team

Purple teaming

Think of them as the coach who gets red and blue in the same room so both get better.

  • Run attacks and watch if defenders catch them
  • Turn every miss into a new detection
  • Share lessons across both teams
See their paths ↓

GRC & security architecture

Governance, risk, compliance & architecture

Think of them as the planners and referees who make sure everything follows the rules and fits together.

  • Measure risk and decide what to fix first
  • Check that security controls actually work
  • Turn tech risks into plain-language plans
See their paths ↓

Other paths

Other paths that aren’t cyber

Think of them as the people who build and run the tech everyone else protects.

  • Write code, build networks, and work with data
  • Help coworkers and customers with tech problems
  • Explain tricky things in plain words

Blue team

Cyber Defense

Blue teams defend the company. They watch for threats, investigate incidents, and protect accounts, systems, and the cloud.

Red & purple team

Offensive Security

Red teams test defenses the way an attacker would, with permission. Purple teams bring red and blue together so offense makes defense better.

GRC & security architecture

Governance, risk, compliance & architecture

GRC teams manage risk, make sure the company follows laws and standards, and security architects design how it all fits together. You don’t need to be a manager to work here.
