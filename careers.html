Teams 101

Meet the Teams

In cybersecurity you’ll hear about the red, blue, and purple teams. Here’s who does what, then explore every path below.

01

Red Team

Friendly hackers you hire, with permission, to test your locks before a real attacker shows up.

  • Try to break in the way an attacker would
  • Find weak spots first
  • Write up how to fix them
See the paths ↓

02

Blue Team

The security guards and detectives. They watch, investigate, and stop attacks.

  • Watch alerts for anything suspicious
  • Investigate odd activity
  • Stop an attack and clean up
See the paths ↓

03

Purple Team

Red and blue working together, so what attackers find makes the defense better.

  • Run attacks and see if they’re caught
  • Turn every miss into a new alert
  • Share lessons across both teams
See the paths ↓
