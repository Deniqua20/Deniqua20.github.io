About this conversation

These original questions explore helping people, investigating, organizing, building, creating, and explaining. Each choice supports two interests. We compare the pattern across your answers with the activities involved in different kinds of work. Four answered questions are needed for a suggestion, along with repeated support for both main interests in that kind of work. Ties are kept together, and a broad or uncertain pattern is described honestly.

The connections are editorial guidance informed by O*NET work activities and NIST NICE role descriptions. This is not the O*NET instrument. For an established assessment with published research, explore the O*NET Interest Profiler.
